TRACE: Targeted Ranking-Aware Counterfactual Explanation

September 22, 2026

Sequential recommender systems use the order of user interactions to predict the next item, but it is difficult to explain why a particular item was recommended.

TRACE studies ranking-aware counterfactual explanation. The search is divided into influence-guided position selection, plausibility-aware candidate retrieval, and actual-margin beam pruning.
